aarón montoya-moraga (they/them) is a chilean person. since 2023 aarón is an assistant professor at the design school at universidad diego portales, teaching the electronic machines and the computational machines studio courses. since 2025 aarón is a phd student at universidad de santiago de chile, researching about design of open source modular sound instruments.
